# Is Vanguard good to buy if you’re worried about the USA? — Oct 8, 2026

Not financial advice. Clarifies vehicle vs country risk for Niloo’s cash plans.

## Short answer
**Yes — Vanguard ETFs are fine to buy.** The company (low-cost index funds, Canadian-listed tickers like VFV / VCN / VXC / VEQT) is not what you’re afraid of.  
What you’re afraid of is **owning a lot of the US stock market**. That’s a fair worry — and you already own a lot of US via NVDA, GOOGL, CRWD, ORCL, etc.

## Two different things people mix up

| Worry | Reality |
|-------|---------|
| “Is Vanguard safe / legit?” | For normal long-term investing in Canada, **VFV / VCN / VXC / VEQT are standard, liquid, low-fee vehicles**. Risk is mostly market risk, not “Vanguard blows up.” |
| “Is the USA a scary place to invest right now?” | Possible — politics, rates, valuations, concentration in mega-tech. That’s **US equity risk**, whether you buy VFV, SPY, or Nvidia. |

Buying **VFV** is usually *less* scary than buying more NVDA/CRWD/GOOGL, because VFV owns ~500 companies, not one story.

## If you’re nervous about the USA — still buy Vanguard, just tilt the mix

You don’t need to abandon US forever. Shrink the US *index* slice and fund Canada + international instead.

### Softer US path for the next ~$2,000 (either account)
| Priority | Ticker | ~$ of 2,000 | Why |
|----------|--------|------------:|-----|
| 1 | **VCN.TO** | 900 | Canada — not dependent on US headlines |
| 2 | **XEF.TO** (or more **VXC.TO**) | 700 | Developed international (Europe/Japan etc.) |
| 3 | **VFV.TO** | 400 | Keep a *small* US core so you don’t go to zero USA |

Skip with this mood: extra CRWD, NVDA, SMH, CEG, single-name US growth.

### What *not* to do from fear
- Don’t park everything in cash long-term (inflation + missed compounding).  
- Don’t sell Vanguard *because the name is American* while keeping Nvidia/CrowdStrike — that’s backwards.  
- Don’t replace VFV with more US single stocks “to be safer.”
