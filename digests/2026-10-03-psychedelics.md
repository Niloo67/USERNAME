## What CMPS actually is

**Compass Pathways (NASDAQ: CMPS)** develops **COMP360**, a synthetic psilocybin treatment for **treatment-resistant depression (TRD)** — depression that didn’t get better on standard antidepressants.

**Why it’s the sector leader right now:**
- Two Phase 3 trials reported positive data (rapid onset, durability out to ~26 weeks / longer follow-up).
- Rolling **FDA NDA** underway; company says final submission still on track for **Q4 2026**.
- Targeting a possible US launch in **H1 2027** — *if* FDA approves **and** DEA/states reschedule (psilocybin is Schedule I today).
- Cash ~**$433M** (as of mid-2026) — runway guided into **2028**, so they’re not immediately desperate for cash.
- Stock ~**$12** recently; up a lot over ~1 year, soft the last month.

**What still has to go right (the risks):**
1. FDA actually approves (delay / CRL / label limits possible).
2. DEA + state rescheduling after approval.
3. Clinics, therapists, and insurers adopt a multi-hour treatment model (like Spravato-style interventional psychiatry).
4. First commercial launch execution — Compass has never sold a drug before.
5. Single-asset concentration: most of the story is COMP360.

**Verdict on CMPS:** Best *pure-play* psychedelic stock if you want this theme. Still a **binary regulatory/commercial bet**, not a quality compounder like GOOGL/MSFT.

## Is now a good time for CMPS?

**Bull case (why people buy):**  
Clinical package looks de-risked vs earlier years; NDA completion + potential 2027 launch is a real catalyst window; cash runway into 2028; category (psychedelic medicine) getting more regulatory attention.

**Bear case (why people wait/skip):**  
Stock already re-rated a lot over the past year; next 6–12 months are all about FDA/DEA/commercial execution; soft last month suggests some profit-taking into the filing; still zero revenue.

**Practical take:**  
- If you want the theme → **small CMPS** is the cleanest expression.  
- If you want “must buy today” quality → still **core ETFs + GOOGL/MSFT**, not CMPS.  
- Don’t size CMPS like a mega-cap. Treat it like a speculative biotech position you’re willing to lose most of.
