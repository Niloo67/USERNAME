# World themes → what to buy (Oct 3, 2026)

Not financial advice. Same idea as “war → oil”: map **what’s happening** to **bottlenecks you can own** — then size them inside your ~10% risk sleeve (keep ~90% boring core).

## How to think about this

| World thing | What gets scarce / expensive | Easy way to own it | Chase risk right now? |
|-------------|------------------------------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Middle East conflict / shipping risk | Oil, diesel, energy security | Energy ETFs / CDN oil | Medium — oil already had big moves |
| AI boom | Memory, chips, then **power** | MU (small) / SMH / nuclear-power names | Memory elevated; **power** less crowded |
| AI + electrification | Copper for grids/data centers | COPX / TECK.B | Softer lately → better entry than chips |
| Rearmament / NATO spend | Defense, missiles, cyber | ITA ETF / CRWD | Defense soft recently; cyber hot |
| Sticky inflation + geopolitics | Gold (sometimes) | GLD / WPM.TO | Soft month — not a must chase |

**Rule:** Buy the **bottleneck**, not the headline. After a spike (oil at $100, SNDK ripping), wait or size tiny.

---

## Theme 1 — War / energy security → oil & Canadian energy

**What’s going on:** Middle East tensions, Hormuz/shipping risk, diesel/product tightness keep an energy-security premium in crude.

**What to buy (if you want the theme):**
- **Easiest:** `XLE` (US energy ETF) or keep Canada equity (`VCN`/`XIC` already has energy banks).
- **CDN single names:** `SU.TO` (Suncor) or `CNQ.TO` (Canadian Natural) — CAD-listed, matches your geography.
- **Bigger integrated:** `XOM` / `CVX` if you prefer US mega-caps.

**Right now:** Oil is still elevated (WTI ~$90s, Brent ~$100+) but whippy. Good as a **small diversifier**, bad as “all-in after the spike.” Cap energy satellite at ~1–2% of portfolio if you add beyond your Canada ETF.

---

## Theme 2 — AI raises hardware / RAM / chip costs → memory & semis

**What’s going on:** AI data centers eat GPUs **and** memory (HBM/DRAM/NAND). Micron just posted a monster quarter; shortage narrative still alive.

**What to buy:**
- **Memory bottleneck:** `MU` (best single name we already researched) — optional, small.
- **Basket:** `SMH` or `SOXX` (semiconductor ETFs) — cleaner than picking one hot chip stock.
- **Skip chase:** `SNDK` after parabolic runs; `NVDA` only if you’re OK owning what’s already widely owned.

**Right now:** Theme is real; prices are **not cheap**. Prefer ETF (`SMH`) or tiny `MU` over loading more after big 2026 runs.

---

## Theme 3 — AI’s *next* bottleneck: electricity / nuclear / grid ⭐ (under-owned vs chips)

**What’s going on:** Hyperscalers (MSFT/META/AMZN) are signing long nuclear and power deals. Industry chatter: chips aren’t the only limit anymore — **grid access and baseload power** are.

**What to buy:**
- **Nuclear generator with real contracts:** `CEG` (Constellation) — often cited as signing multi-year PPAs with Big Tech.
- **Uranium fuel chain:** `CCJ` (Cameco, Canadian) or `URA` (uranium miners ETF).
- **Canada utility ballast:** `H.TO` (Hydro One) — more regulated/slow, less “AI lottery,” still grid exposure.

**Right now:** This is one of the **better “world theme” adds** vs chasing another chip stock — structural, multi-year, less consensus than NVDA/MU. Still volatile; size ~1–2%.

---

## Theme 4 — AI + grids + EVs → copper

**What’s going on:** Copper is the metal for electrification, data centers, and defense. Supply is slow to add (mines take years).

**What to buy:**
- **ETF:** `COPX` (global copper miners).
- **CDN name:** `TECK.B.TO` / `TECK` (Teck) — copper-focused Canadian exposure.

**Right now:** Softer over the past month while the long thesis stayed intact — often a cleaner add than oil/chips after spikes. Good **1–2%** diversifier in the risk sleeve.

---

## Theme 5 — Rearmament / conflict → defense & cyber

**What’s going on:** Higher NATO/G7 defense spending; more money into missiles, drones, battlefield AI, and cyber.

**What to buy:**
- **Defense basket:** `ITA` or `XAR` (aerospace/defense ETFs) — easier than picking Lockheed vs Raytheon.
- **Cyber (AI + security):** `CRWD` or `PANW` — but they’ve already run hard lately; buy dips, don’t chase green days.

**Right now:** Traditional defense ETFs have been soft recently (possible better entry). Cyber is hot — size carefully.

---

## Theme 6 — Fear / inflation shocks → gold (optional)

**What’s going on:** Geopolitics + high yields = gold is inconsistent. Soft recently.

**What to buy:** `GLD` / `IAU`, or CDN miners `WPM.TO` / `AEM.TO` (higher risk than GLD).

**Right now:** Optional hedge only (~0–2%). Not a primary “world theme” buy this week.
