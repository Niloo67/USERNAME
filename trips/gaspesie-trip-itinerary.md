# Gaspésie Road Trip — Montréal → Percé

**When:** Late August or early–mid September (prefer early–mid Sept for fewer crowds + better hotel rates)  
**Length:** 6–7 days  
**Style:** Car trip, decent mid-range hotels (not luxury)

> **Gannet tip:** Be in Percé before late September if the Île Bonaventure colony is a priority — birds start leaving then. Boats usually run into early October.

---

## Final recommended route

| Day | Route | Drive | Sleep |
|-----|--------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Montréal → **Matane** *(or Rimouski)* | ~7 h (Matane) / ~6 h (Rimouski) | Matane or Rimouski |
| 2 | → Percé | ~5 h from Matane / ~6.5 h from Rimouski | Percé |
| 3–5 | Percé base (3–4 nights) | local / day trips | Percé |
| 6 | Percé → **Rivière-du-Loup** | ~7–7.5 h | Rivière-du-Loup |
| 7 | → Montréal *(optional Kamouraska stop)* | ~4–4.5 h | — |

### Why these stops?
- **Matane (outbound):** slightly better than Rimouski — shorter Day 2 into Percé, solid hotels
- **Rimouski (alt outbound):** keep if you want **Parc national du Bic** (seals + sunset)
- **Rivière-du-Loup (return):** practical hotels/restaurants; stop in **Kamouraska** for sunset or morning coffee
- **Kamouraska alone:** charming but tiny — better as a stop than the only lodging base

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1 — Montréal → Matane (or Rimouski)
Leave early. Easy lunch stop: Québec City or Rivière-du-Loup.

**If Rimouski:**
- **Parc national du Bic** — seals at Pointe aux Épinettes, Pic Champlain views, sunset
- **Pointe-au-Père maritime site** — lighthouse, Onondaga submarine, *Empress of Ireland* museum

**If Matane:** settle in, dinner by the water, rest for Percé day.

---

### Day 2 — Drive to Percé
Scenic **Route 132**. Break every 1.5–2 hours.

Worth short stops:
- Sainte-Flavie / Matane stretch — river views
- **Gaspé town** — fuel, groceries, last bigger services before Percé

Arrive late afternoon: boardwalk + **Rocher Percé** at golden hour.

---

### Days 3–5 — Percé (must-sees)

#### Day 3 — The icons (weather-dependent — do first)
1. **Boat to Île Bonaventure** (Sépaq) — #1 activity  
   - Close pass by Percé Rock  
   - Northern gannet colony (~110,000+ birds)  
   - Book boat tickets ahead
2. Walk quai / beach for rock viewpoints from town

#### Day 4 — Heights & trails
1. **Géoparc mondial UNESCO de Percé** — glass platform (~200 m), Tektonik exhibit, Mont Sainte-Anne trails
2. **Parc de la Rivière Émeraude** (Coin-du-Banc) — waterfall + emerald water
3. Optional: **L’Anse-à-Beaufils** harbour / historic general store

#### Day 5 — Day trip: Forillon National Park (~1–1.25 h each way)
- **Cap Bon-Ami** — cliffs, seabirds, seals
- **Mont Saint-Alban** fire tower — best 360° view
- Optional shorter coastal sections of **Les Graves** toward Cap-Gaspé
- Parks Canada entry fee applies

**Rain/fog backup:** Géoparc indoor exhibits, Gaspé museums, microbreweries, Cap d’Espoir drive.

---

### Day 6 — Percé → Rivière-du-Loup
Long driving day. Aim to arrive for sunset.

Optional: short stop in Kamouraska if timing works (~30–40 min west of Rivière-du-Loup is wrong direction — Kamouraska is ~45 min **west** toward Montréal, so better as next-morning stop).

---

### Day 7 — Home to Montréal
- Optional **Kamouraska** morning stop: village walk, tidal flats, coffee/bakery
- Drive Montréal ~4–4.5 h from Rivière-du-Loup

---

## 6-day compressed version
Same route, only **3 nights in Percé**:
- Combine Géoparc + Rivière Émeraude into one day
- Either skip Forillon or do Cap Bon-Ami only
- Still do Bonaventure on the first full Percé day

## Hotel suggestions

Rough shoulder-season ballpark: **CAD $150–250/night** before tax. September often cheaper than late August.

### Outbound — Matane (preferred)
| Hotel | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| **Pavillon sur Mer / Riôtel Matane** | Pool, sea views, strong reviews — top pick |
| **Quality Inn Matane** | Breakfast included, solid value |

## Where to eat in Percé
- **La Maison du Pêcheur** — top seafood; reserve
- **La Boîte à Fruits de Mer** — lobster rolls, casual terrace
- **Paqbo** (Riotel) — convenient waterfront
- **La Vieille Usine** (L’Anse-à-Beaufils) — solid local seafood, less touristy

---

## Practical tips
1. Book **Bonaventure boat + Percé hotel first**, then outbound / return nights
2. Fill gas in Gaspé / bigger towns — stations thin out
3. Fog is common in Percé — keep Bonaventure flexible (swap days)
4. Pack layers for September (wind at lookouts)
5. Check tide tables for Bic seals and Percé beach walks
6. Prefer **Kamouraska** for scenery; **Québec City** for a lively last night; **Rivière-du-Loup** for the best logistics middle ground

---

## Booking target
Leave Montréal **Thu/Fri** of the last week of August, or **first two weekends of September**, with **4 nights Percé** so weather doesn’t wipe out Bonaventure or Forillon.
