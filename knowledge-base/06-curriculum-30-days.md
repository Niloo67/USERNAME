# Reading & follow curriculum (30 days)

A practical plan to build real investing literacy while the digest agent runs. ~2–3 hours/week.

## Week 1 — Operating system

| Do | Link |
|----|------|
| Read Bogleheads start-up kit | https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Bogleheads%C2%AE_investing_start-up_kit |
| Watch 2 Ben Felix videos (asset allocation / behavior) | https://www.youtube.com/@benfelixcsi |
| Skim Damodaran data page once (don’t memorize) | https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/ |

**Outcome:** you can explain why VTI+VXUS+bonds beats random stock tips.

## Week 2 — How markets are measured

| Do | Link |
|----|------|
| Bookmark FRED (10Y yield, CPI, unemployment) | https://fred.stlouisfed.org |
| Learn Finviz map (sectors) | https://finviz.com/map.ashx |
| One Plain Bagel video on ETFs vs stocks | YouTube: The Plain Bagel |

**Outcome:** you can read a digest’s “regime snapshot” without confusion.

## Week 3 — Valuation & skepticism

| Do | Link |
|----|------|
| Damodaran intro valuation lecture (any recent) | YouTube: Aswath Damodaran |
| One Patrick Boyle video on a hyped product/theme | YouTube: Patrick Boyle |
| Read our signal philosophy | [`04-signal-philosophy.md`](04-signal-philosophy.md) |

**Outcome:** you know why Trends/X ≠ a buy signal.

## Week 4 — Your life stage (30s)

| Do | Link |
|----|------|
| Money Guy FOO overview | YouTube: The Money Guy Show |
| Rob Berger on portfolio tracking / simplicity | YouTube: Rob Berger |
| Re-read allocation policy | [`05-architecture.md`](05-architecture.md) |

**Outcome:** digests become *nudges* inside a plan, not the plan itself.

## Ongoing (forever)

- **Weekly:** listen to 1 Rational Reminder episode *or* skim one institutional outlook (JPM/Amundi) and ask: “does price confirm?”
- **Monthly:** open `digests/`, mark 3 ideas right/wrong, update Memories or a KB note
- **Quarterly:** rebalance brokerage toward target sleeves; ignore weekly noise

## Books (optional, high ROI)

1. *The Bogleheads’ Guide to Investing* (Larimore / Lindauer / LeBoeuf)  
2. *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing* (John Bogle)  
3. *A Random Walk Down Wall Street* (Burton Malkiel) — markets are hard to beat  
4. Damodaran’s *Narrative and Numbers* — if you buy individual stocks  

## Anti-curriculum (do not binge)

- Daily “top 10 stocks” YouTube  
- Options “income” lifestyle channels  
- Leveraged ETF “double your money” content  
- Paid Discord signal rooms  

If a creator needs you anxious every day to stay subscribed, they are optimizing engagement — not your 30-year outcome.
