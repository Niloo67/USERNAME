### A3. Watch list — good businesses, wrong price or wrong moment

- **ETN / PWR / GEV** — demand is unambiguous (Eaton data-center orders +240%, GEV turbine slots sold into 2031), but at 29x / 33x / 40x+ you are paying for 3+ years of it. Buy on a further 10–15% pullback or a post-earnings reset. Not "must-buy."
- **COPX / FCX / TECK** — I am **downgrading copper from "buy" to "watch."** Copper is at a record (~$14,700/t). Morningstar's Oct 12 note puts copper miners 15–120% *above* fair value (FCX fair value $32 vs $69 price). Macquarie sees a 700kt/yr *surplus* from 2027; UBS sees a 379kt *deficit*. When the best forecasters disagree that much and the price is at a record, that is a coin-flip, not a must-buy. If you want copper, TECK (15.4x) is cheaper than FCX (17.7x).
- **MSFT** — great company, +31% in 3 months, near high. Wait for after Oct 28.
- **MU** — 6x forward is the cheapest multiple on the list, but it is 6x *peak-cycle* earnings; Citi now sees DRAM/NAND prices peaking in 2Q27 and cut its target to $1,150. Volatility 73%. A spec-sleeve position only, never core.
- **Gold** — down 23% because real yields are the highest since 2007 and the USD is at a 19-month high. That driver does not reverse until the Fed stops. Central-bank buying is a floor, not a catalyst. 0–5% as insurance if you want it; not a must-buy.
- **Canadian banks / VCN** — up 35–54% in a year, 17.3x vs 14.1x average. Keep the normal DCA, do not add extra.
