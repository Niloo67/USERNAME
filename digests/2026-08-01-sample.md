## Ideas ranked for a balanced-growth mix

1. **VTI (or SPY) — Core hold / drip** — US total market core. Keeps you invested without needing to pick every winner in a rotation market. Risk: US valuation concentration in AI-linked names.
2. **VXUS — Add / overweight vs US-only** — International diversification while US valuations stay elevated; 5d already constructive. Risk: USD and geopolitics.
3. **SCHD — Small quality ballast** — Dividend/quality sleeve for when growth leadership whipsaws. Risk: can lag in melt-up tech weeks.
4. **XLI or industrial AI-infrastructure ETF — Satellite watch / small add (≤3–5%)** — Capex/reshoring/data-center build is a multi-year theme; sector already ran so size small. Risk: valuation after the move.
5. **XLV — Watch for staggered adds** — Defensive + valuation support if AI air pockets hit; not a momentum chase this week. Risk: policy/drug headlines.
6. **Avoid chasing AMZN/MSFT after vertical spikes as “new money” all-in** — Fine to hold if already owned; fresh capital better averaged via VTI/QQQ. Risk: mean-reversion after event-driven jumps.
7. **BND ~10–15% — Keep ballast** — Rates choppy; bonds dampen equity drawdowns without going to cash. Risk: duration if yields rise.
