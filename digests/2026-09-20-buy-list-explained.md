# What looks good to buy — explained list (Sep 20, 2026)

Not financial advice. This is a longer plain-English version of the weekend report so you can actually decide.

## How to read this

Your money has two jobs:

1. **~90% “boring core”** — grow steadily over years (Canada + US + world + some safe ballast).
2. **~10% “risk sleeve”** — try for extra upside (individual stocks). This can swing a lot.

Below: what I’d buy, **why it makes sense right now**, how much, and what could go wrong.

---

## Tier A — Buy with confidence (the core)

### 1) Canada stock ETF — **VCN** or **XIC**
**What it is:** Owns a big basket of Canadian companies (banks, energy, miners, etc.) in one fund.

**Why buy:** This is your “home country” foundation. You live and spend in CAD. Keeping meaningful Canada exposure means you’re not betting your whole future on the US dollar and US tech alone.

**Why now:** Nothing special about this week — that’s the point. Fed hikes and oil spikes shouldn’t stop scheduled contributions to the core.

**Suggested size:** Part of every contribution (often ~20–35% of new money, depending on how Canada-heavy you already are).

**What could go wrong:** Canada can lag when oil falls or banks struggle. That’s normal — don’t abandon it for one soft month.

---

### 2) US stock ETF — **VFV** or **VUN** (CAD-listed) / **VTI** or **SPY** (USD)
**What it is:** Broad US stock market. This is where most long-term growth usually comes from.

**Why buy:** US companies (including the big AI winners) dominate global profits. For a growth-balanced plan, this is the main engine.

**Why now:** Markets digested a Fed rate hike and bounced when oil cooled. You’re not trying to time the perfect day — you’re buying ownership of the US economy on a schedule.

**Suggested size:** Often the largest piece of new contributions (~35–50%).

**What could go wrong:** Higher rates and another Fed hike talk can make US stocks choppy near-term. That’s why this sits in the 90%, not as a one-week trade.

---

### 3) International stocks — **XEF** / **VIU** or **VXUS**
**What it is:** Stocks outside Canada/US (Europe, Japan, etc.).

**Why buy:** When North America is obsessed with oil, tariffs, or the Fed, other countries often move differently. This is insurance against “everything I own is the same bet.”

**Why now:** This week has Trump–Xi / geopolitics headlines. Diversification matters more when the news is noisy.

**Suggested size:** Meaningful but smaller than US (~15–25% of equities).

**What could go wrong:** Intl can lag for years. You’re buying balance, not the hottest ticker.

---

### 4) Short / simple bonds (ballast) — short CAD bond ETF or similar
**What it is:** Safer money that doesn’t swing like stocks. “Short” means it isn’t as sensitive to interest-rate moves as long bonds.

**Why buy:** When stocks get scary, ballast helps you stay invested instead of panic-selling.

**Why now:** The 10-year US yield is still around **5%**. Long bonds (like TLT) can hurt when rates stay high. Short ballast is the safer “shock absorber.”

**Suggested size:** Roughly 10–20% of the whole portfolio.

**What could go wrong:** Bonds won’t make you rich. That’s fine — their job is stability.

---

## Tier B — Best ideas in the ~10% risk sleeve (explained)

### 5) **GOOGL** (Alphabet / Google) — best quality stock to buy in the sleeve right now
**What it is:** Google Search, YouTube, Google Cloud, Gemini AI.

**Why it’s good to buy:**
- The **business** is still strong: cloud growth has been extremely fast, and ads remain a cash machine.
- The **stock** had pulled back earlier on “they’re spending too much on AI.” That created a better entry than buying names that already went parabolic.
- Compared with MSFT right now, GOOGL has looked like the cleaner mega-cap AI/cloud add in recent weeks.

**How to use it:** Make this the **#1 single stock** in your 10% sleeve (for example ~2–3% of your whole portfolio).

**What could go wrong:** AI spending stays huge and free cash flow stays ugly longer than investors want. Regulation / antitrust can also scare the stock. Still a high-quality company — not a meme.

---

### 6) **META** — best “story + momentum” stock, but buy soft days
**What it is:** Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp — plus new AI products (Muse agent with paid plans).

**Why it’s good to buy:**
- Investors have been worried Meta spends too much on AI with no clear consumer product payoff.
- Recent product news (paid AI agent) is the market’s first real “maybe the spending pays” moment.
- It was one of the stronger names in our sleeve research over recent weeks — even after cooling Friday.

**How to use it:** Roughly ~2–3.5% of total portfolio **if** you want short/medium-term upside in the sleeve. Prefer buying when it’s down a bit (soft morning), not after a big green day.

**What could go wrong:** If AI subscriptions disappoint, the stock can give back gains fast. It’s still mostly an ads company. Size it as a satellite, not your life savings.

---

### 7) **MU** (Micron) — optional small memory bet (not a must)
**What it is:** Makes memory chips (DRAM / HBM / NAND) that AI data centers need.

**Why it can be good:**
- AI needs memory badly; shortage + high prices = huge profits for memory makers.
- **Sep 30 earnings** is a real catalyst — guidance will tell us if the boom is still intact.

**Why it’s only “optional / small”:**
- Memory stocks are violent. They can drop 10% on a “good” report if guidance disappoints.
- It already bounced hard late last week.

**How to use it:** At most ~1–2% of total portfolio if you want this theme — or wait until after earnings if you hate event risk.

**What could go wrong:** Margins peak and mean-revert. China supply / capacity adds. Classic boom-bust cycle returns.

## Tier C — Don’t buy (or only tiny if you already love the name)

### 8) **SNDK** (SanDisk) — skip for new money right now
**Why people like it:** Pure AI storage / NAND story — same world as MU.

**Why I’m saying skip:** Last week it had a massive one-day jump. Buying after that kind of spike is usually how people overpay. If you already own a little, you can hold a tiny piece. If you don’t own it, **MU is the cleaner memory vehicle**.

---

### 9) **MSFT** — fine to own, not the best fresh buy for the 10% sleeve
**Why it’s great long-term:** Azure, Office, Copilot — fortress business.

**Why it’s not the top buy *right now* for extra risk money:** The stock has been near highs / sticky while the market frets about huge AI spending and delayed free cash flow. For “use the 10% to try to make money,” GOOGL and META have looked like better setups lately.

**Where it belongs:** If you want more Microsoft, put it in the quality/core side — not as your main short-term sleeve bet.

---

### 10) Energy stocks (**SU**, **CNQ**, **XLE**) — skip for now
**Why they worked before:** Oil spiked toward/above $100 on geopolitics.

**Why skip now:** Oil then fell hard. Buying energy after a spike that already reversed is late. Keep energy exposure mainly through your Canada ETF if you want any.
