## Logistics checklist (accuracy > hype)

Before any “buy now” decision:

1. **Price vs story** — Does the last 1m/3m move already embed the news? (Energy: yes. Semis: partially unwound.)
2. **Catalyst calendar** — CPI/PPI, FOMC, oil headlines, mega-cap earnings/capex comments.
3. **Position size** — Core ETFs get the money; single names ≤1–3% each; thematic ETFs ≤5%.
4. **Overlap** — VTI+QQQ+XLK+SMH = hidden mega-cap stack. Prefer VTI+QQQ+SCHD+XLE/XLF/XLV.
5. **Data sources to wire into the Automation** — Trends MCP (search interest), FinancialMCP (fundamentals/news), X sentiment (hype filter), then email digests.
6. **Rebalance rule** — Quarterly; trim any satellite that doubles its target weight after a spike.
